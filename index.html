HOW TO COUNT

+12 · 3 · 4 · 5 · 6 (low cards gone = good)
07 · 8 · 9 (neutral)
−110 · J · Q · K · A (high cards gone = bad)

Running count: add up those values for every card you see.

True count: running count ÷ decks remaining. Bet more & deviate as it climbs.

The goal: keep the running count in your head without slowing down. That's what these drills build.
