Plumb

A hanging mobile, missing some of its weights. A bar hangs level only when the weight on one side times its arm equals the weight on the other times its arm — and every bar hangs from another bar, so nothing settles until all of it does. Hang a weight and watch it swing through: bars tip the moment the load changes, and the whole mobile below them goes with it.
