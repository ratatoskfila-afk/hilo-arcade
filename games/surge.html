Surge

Drag anywhere to move, double tap to discharge.Move with WASD, the arrows, or by dragging. Space discharges. Discharging destroys everything the blast reaches and banks your charge, worth far more the more it kills at once. Blocks shield them from the blast, and you crawl in the violet fields — which only matters when you are heavy. Three lives: getting caught costs whatever you were carrying and puts you back at the start of the level, not the start of the run.

Turn your phone sideways — the arena is 8:5, so landscape gives you much more room to run.
